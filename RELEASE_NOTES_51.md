# Noor Release 51

Incremental update on Release 50. Adds seven Quran stories with sourced verse chapters and distinct illustrations, chapter reading resume, fiqh lessons and a timed ten-question quiz, tadabbur, a simplified home banner, clearer Arabic question input and referenced local answers, varied Quran order rounds, atlas controls, and Android foreground A–B repetition.

The APK retains the existing debug-signing distribution approach. Quran data and Amiri fonts are preserved. Local AI interpretation remains optional; deterministic Quran/reference searches do not require paid APIs. Religious summaries identify sources and areas of madhhab differences. Map routes and illustrations are explanatory representations.

Responsive browser and Android emulator results are attached to the build under Noor-QA-51. This release is published only if required checks pass. Background playback depends on accessible or downloaded audio; Android force-stop ends playback.
